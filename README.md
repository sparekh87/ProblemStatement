PROBLEM STATEMENT 
Retail banking customers often struggle to navigate complex investment options and financial products tailored to their 
unique goals and risk profiles. Current advisory services are limited by manual processes, inconsistent advice, and 
lack of real-time data integration, leading to suboptimal investment decisions. Banks face challenges scaling 
personalized financial advisory due to resource constraints and compliance requirements. Customers demand 
instant, accurate, and personalized recommendations that consider market trends, account data, and regulatory 
constraints. Existing robo-advisors frequently lack multi-source data integration and advanced conversational 
capabilities, limiting engagement and trust. The volume of customers requiring guidance is growing rapidly, especially 
among digitally native users expecting seamless omni-channel experiences. Failure to address this gap risks customer 
attrition and lost revenue opportunities. A scalable AI-driven solution that integrates multiple data sources and delivers 
personalized investment plans can enhance customer satisfaction, increase assets under management, and reduce 
advisory costs. 
Data Considerations: 
Customer demographic and financial profile data in structured formats from internal banking systems; real-time 
market data feeds from financial APIs (e.g., Bloomberg, Alpha Vantage); historical investment product performance 
datasets; regulatory compliance rules encoded as decision logic; anonymized transaction histories for behavior 
analysis; data quality checks for completeness and freshness; privacy compliance with GDPR and banking regulations; 
synthetic customer profiles and market scenarios for testing; preprocessing includes normalization and entity 
extraction for documents. 
Consider using synthetic or anonymized data where appropriate. Ensure data quality and relevance to the problem 
context. 
SOLUTION EXPECTATIONS 
A web-based AI assistant featuring a multi-agent conversational interface capable of understanding customer goals 
and risk tolerance, generating personalized investment strategies by integrating live market and account data, and 
embedding compliance validations. The system should include dashboards for advisors to monitor recommendation 
accuracy and customer engagement metrics. Key integrations include banking CRM APIs, financial market data 
providers, and compliance rule engines. Success is measured by recommendation relevance, dialogue completion 
rates, and system responsiveness. The prototype should demonstrate end-to-end scenario handling from customer 
query to actionable plan delivery with audit trails.
